## Consulting Overview

Statistical design of experiments (DoE) is critical for researchers to draw valid causal conclusions while minimizing experimental error and treatment replication costs.

As a statistical consultant for animal science (*Fakultas Peternakan IPB*) and food science researchers, this project handled experimental modeling, assumption validation, and hypothesis testing for client research datasets.

---

## Statistical Framework

1. **Experimental Design Structures**:
   - **Completely Randomized Design (CRD / RAL)**: Applied to laboratory trials with homogeneous environmental conditions (e.g. evaluating antioxidant capacity across Sambiloto-Coffee-Chocolate herbal formulation ratios).
   - **Randomized Complete Block Design (RCBD / RAK)**: Applied to animal feed trials accounting for animal weight/age batch variance.
2. **Hypothesis Testing & Diagnostics**:
   - One-Way and Two-Way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA).
   - Variance homogeneity verification (Levene's Test) and normality of residuals (Shapiro-Wilk Test).
   - Post-hoc pairwise mean comparisons via **Tukey's Honestly Significant Difference (HSD)** test ($\alpha = 0.05$).
