## Certification Background

This project represents the competency portfolio executed for the **BNSP Data Analyst Professional Certification** issued by *LSP TIK Global* (2026).

The assessment validated end-to-end competency in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, hypothesis testing, statistical modeling, and executive dashboard communication across real-world case studies.

---

## Key Case Studies

### 1. Industrial Safety, Health & Environment (SHE) Analytics
- **Context**: Incident log analysis for mining operations (PT Banana Coal).
- **Analysis**: Categorized workplace incident frequencies, near-miss report distributions, and seasonal hazard patterns.
- **Outcome**: Generated a corrective risk mitigation priority matrix identifying high-risk equipment operating shifts.

### 2. Generative AI Impact on Student Academic Performance
- **Context**: Multi-variable survey exploring AI tool adoption and academic productivity among higher education students.
- **Analysis**: Conducted Chi-Square tests of independence, bivariate correlations, and multiple regression in SPSS/R to assess whether AI usage frequency correlates with self-reported GPA and study duration.
